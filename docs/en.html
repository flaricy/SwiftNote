A NOTE. A PURCHASE. A MOMENT.

A time clue for every purchase.

Write down what you bought and what it cost. Click that line to see when it was last edited. When you write as you order, that timestamp gives you a useful clue later.

Click a line → see its time

The timestamp is the line’s latest edit time. Editing it again updates the time; it does not preserve the original order time or read a merchant’s order details.
